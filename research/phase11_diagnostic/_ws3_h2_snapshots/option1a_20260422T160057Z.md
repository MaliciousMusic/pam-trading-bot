## Cross-check: dropped-fraction vs directional-hit

Option 1a's `last_dropped_fraction` is the wrong-direction share of non-zero pairs in the window, computed at the **last** refit. The `directional_hit` column is the per-anchor hit rate from `ForecastAuditor` over the full day (not a trailing window). They only need to match in distribution, not value; a large divergence would imply a dataflow problem. Under `MIN_PRED_BPS=0.5` the two are expected within a few %.
